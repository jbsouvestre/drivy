How to drive

  • Wor↑ Accelerate
  • Sor↓ Brake hold to reverse
  • ADor←→ Steer
  • Space Handbrake swing the tail out and drift
  • Q Chime curious animals turn to look — great for portraits
  • L Headlights on / off — they switch on by themselves at dusk
  • C Camera look with the mouse, scroll to zoom, click or Space to snap
  • J Field journal every animal you've photographed
  • T Fast-forward time hold to speed up the day
  • M Nature sounds mute / unmute the ambience
  • E Honk! hold for a longer toot
  • H Show these controls pauses the game
  • Esc Back to the menu

🐾 Animals notice a fast car and show a ? when they're getting nervous. Slow down or stop and they'll calm down. Creep up for close shots, and ring the chime for a portrait.

✨ Drift tip: tap Space as you turn in, then steer the other way to hold it.
